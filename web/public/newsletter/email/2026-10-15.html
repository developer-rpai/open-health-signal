Strongest prespecified safety signals

Evans

TNF inhibitors · Tuberculosis

PRR 7.54 (95% CI 7.28–7.82) · ROR 7.56 (95% CI 7.29–7.84)

Tuberculosis was reported more often than expected with TNF inhibitors than with all other FAERS reports (PRR 7.54, 95% CI 7.28–7.82). This is a disproportionality signal. The reporting odds ratio is 7.56 (95% CI 7.29–7.84). This meets the Evans screen: at least 3 reports, a PRR of at least 2, and a chi-square of at least 4. The ratio is not an incidence rate.
