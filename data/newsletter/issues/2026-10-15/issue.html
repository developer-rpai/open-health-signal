Inaugural briefing · 15 October 2026

TNF inhibitors: tuberculosis signal, Tyenne biosimilar recall

This week

  • Tuberculosis was reported more often than expected with TNF inhibitors (PRR 7.54).
  • Tyenne, a tocilizumab biosimilar, is under a Class I recall for presence of particulate matter.
  • FDA added warnings for heart events, cancer, clots and death to JAK inhibitors.
  • A safety trial found a higher risk of heart problems and cancer with Xeljanz.
  • Imuldosa, an ustekinumab biosimilar, is under a Class II recall for lack of assurance of sterility.
